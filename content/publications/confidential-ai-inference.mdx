# Uncover Individual Identity: Confidential AI Inference

Exploring confidential AI inference techniques to safeguard individual identities in data processing. As AI becomes more integrated into our lives, maintaining privacy is paramount.

## Deep Dive

This paper outlines the technical challenges and solutions for performing inference on sensitive data without exposing the underlying identities.

- Privacy-preserving architectures.
- Differential privacy in large scale inference.
- Hardware-backed security for AI workloads.
