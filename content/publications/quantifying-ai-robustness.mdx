# Quantifying AI Model Robustness

How do we know if an AI model is truly secure? This research provides a mathematical framework for quantifying robustness against evasion.

## Key Metrics

We introduce several new metrics to evaluate the reliability of model output under adversarial perturbations.

- Robustness index.
- Evasion probability density.
- Adversarial distance mapping.
