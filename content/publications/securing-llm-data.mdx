# Securing the LLM Data Supply Chain

Data is the lifeblood of AI. Ensuring its integrity from collection to training is a critical security challenge.

## Overview

We outline the risks of data poisoning and unauthorized access during the pre-training phase.

## Recommendations

- Provenance tracking for datasets.
- Automated integrity verification.
- Secure enclaves for data processing.
