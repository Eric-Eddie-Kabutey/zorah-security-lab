# Track Lost Phones: GPT-5 Cybersecurity

As frontier AI models get more powerful, the stakes for secure deployment get higher. This publication explores how GPT-5 can be used to enhance cybersecurity measures, specifically in the context of protecting against emerging threats and locating lost devices through advanced intelligence.

## Overview

In collaboration with OpenAI, we evaluated the potential of GPT-5 to assist in complex cybersecurity operations. Our focus was on identifying vulnerabilities and developing robust defense mechanisms.

## Key Findings

- Enhanced predictive capabilities for threat detection.
- Improved precision in identifying compromised assets.
- New frameworks for automated response strategies.
